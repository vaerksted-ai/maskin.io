AI-Native Loop Sprint

AI agents running self-learning loops. Outcomes, not prompts.

Your team stops prompting one message at a time. You build loops where agents run, learn, and deliver outcomes — in the same workspace as the humans who guide them.

In the AI-Native Loop Sprint, you set up your first self-learning loops in half a day to a day, then run them alongside our team on Slack for the rest of the sprint. Every loop learns from what it just shipped, so quality climbs while you watch. By the end, your loops have graduated from dry run to either semi-autonomous or fully autonomous — delivering outcomes on their own, with your team stepping in only for the calls that matter.

What kinds of loops teams run

Loops that ship every week.

Eight loop shapes teams start with in a sprint. As the marketplace fills out, each row links to its own listing.

  • Shared business context (LLM knowledge wiki) one shared context your agents and your team both read, fed by meetings, docs, competitor scans, and Slack.
  • Product discovery customer feedback, tickets, and interviews cluster into raw opportunities.
  • Opportunities to strategic bets those opportunities get shaped into bets with a close condition, ready to ship.
  • AI coding delivery shaped bets ship inside Maskin with coding agents, or hand off to Linear or Jira.
  • Go-to-market using LinkedIn in-ICP signal fires a research-and-draft loop; humans gate the send.
  • New customer activation onboarding that adapts to what the customer signed up for and drives to the first outcome.
  • Customer success in Slack a forward-deployed agent watching behaviour and analytics, drafting the right next step, escalating the calls that matter.
  • SEO inbound marketing marketing pages that end when they rank, not when they publish.

Bring your own loop shape too — any weekly bottleneck your team keeps hitting is a loop candidate.

How the sprint runs

Half a day to a day of setup. Then it runs.

  1. Intro call.

    You name the weekly bottleneck. We agree which loops the sprint will set up.

  2. Setup session.

    Half a day to a full day — we set up the loops with your team, on your stack, with your models.

  3. Sprint window.

    Your team has direct Slack access to ours while the loops run. We hold follow-up sessions to review outputs, tighten close conditions, and lift the loops out of dry run.

  4. Close.

    Every loop has graduated to semi-autonomous or fully autonomous — based on what it's learned during the sprint — and is delivering outcomes with your team stepping in only for the calls that matter.

What “graduated” means

Every loop earns its own trust level.

Every loop starts in dry run — producing under supervision, every output critiqued.

When the loop's quality holds against its close condition, it graduates to:

semi-autonomous

The loop runs on its own, but every material call goes through a human gate before it lands.

fully autonomous

The loop runs on its own end-to-end, with a human sampling outputs and stepping in when the close condition drifts.

Loops that stop meeting their close condition get demoted back to dry run automatically. Trust is earned per loop, on evidence — not on a calendar, not on a demo.

Questions

Questions, answered.

How long is the sprint?

Setup is half a day to a full day. The sprint window runs after that, with Slack access and follow-up sessions until every loop has graduated. We scope the exact length on the intro call.

What does it cost?

Named on the intro call, tailored to the sprint scope. We don't publish a rate because the scope moves with your ambition.

Can we self-host?

Yes. Apache 2.0. You run it on your own infrastructure and pay your model provider directly.

Can we bring our own model?

Yes. Model-agnostic. Route each loop to the model that fits.

Which loops can we start with?

Any of the eight above, or a shape you bring us. Common starts: Product Discovery into Opportunities to Strategic Bets, one go-to-market loop, and Shared Business Context underneath, all feeding each other.

What if a loop doesn't hit its close condition?

It stays in dry run and we keep tightening it in follow-up sessions. Loops that never earn their close condition get retired — you keep the substrate and the learnings.

Do you replace our tracker?

No. Loops can ship inside Maskin with coding agents, or hand off to Linear or Jira. The sale is the layer above your stack, not the tracker.

How is this different from an AI consultant?

A consultant leaves you with a slide deck. We leave you with loops that are still running when we're gone, and a team that can build the next ones on their own.

How many sprints do you take?

Limited slots. Book a meeting or email us to check current availability.
