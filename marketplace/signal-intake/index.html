The job

Signal Intake reads public discussions, public posts from people you name, and open-source projects and releases in your area. For example, it files a note when buyers discuss a recurring problem, such as architects retyping site-visit notes into three tools, or when a new project points to a useful pattern.

Replaces: relying on a forwarded thread, a screenshot or a chance mention to hear what is being discussed.

Stays with your team: deciding what matters and what to do about it, such as whether a customer problem should shape the roadmap.

How the flow runs

  1. You name 3 to 6 topics and the people to watch, or confirm a proposed starting list of ten.
  2. Three times a week, Maskin reads public discussions about your topics and public posts and talks from the people you watch.
  3. Once a week, Maskin checks trending open-source projects and new releases in your area.
  4. For each notable idea, Maskin files one short note with a source link, date, confidence rating and lane tag. When the same idea appears in three places, it adds the sources to one note.
  5. Each sweep ends with one summary comment on the flow, grouped by lane, with one linked line per item.
  6. A week with nothing notable and every source readable produces no filler. If a source is blocked or unreachable, Maskin says which source and why.
  7. Each month, Maskin rechecks the watchlist and proposes people to add or drop for a person to confirm.

Amber steps are human approvals.

What you need to run it

  • Name 3 to 6 topics your buyers care about.
  • Confirm the starting watchlist, or name the people to follow.
  • Optional: connect LinkedIn read access for named people’s posts, or YouTube transcript access. Otherwise Maskin uses built-in web search and page reading.
  • See the first sweep’s summary comment, grouped by lane, with source links, or a note if a source could not be read.
  • Notes can go to your Product Discovery flow, which groups them into opportunities. Signal Intake works on its own and works best with that flow.

Trust surface

  • Human gates: you confirm the starting watchlist and any proposed people to add or drop. Your team decides what to do with a note.
  • Your data stays yours: Maskin reads public sources only. It does not dig into private data or engage anywhere.
  • Fails safely: if a source is blocked or unreachable, Maskin names it and says why instead of treating it as a quiet week. Some sites cannot be read.

Example week

Example summary, Monday sweep, for a fictional company, Brightlane (project software for architects).

Source unreachable: one large forum blocked the reader (error 429), so the community lane was partly skipped.

Pain: two threads this week say architects retype site-visit notes into three tools (source links, confidence Medium, 5 comments).

Launch and reaction: a new voice-notes app launched on a launch site. Comments praise setup speed and complain about no export (source link).

Voices: a product leader you watch argues that discovery gets more important as building gets cheaper (source link, filed as one note, adds to an existing note from last week).

Open source: a trending project turns site photos into checklists (source link). Worth a look as a pattern, not a rival.
