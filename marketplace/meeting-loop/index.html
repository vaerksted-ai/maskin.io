Marketplace / The meeting insights flow

Flow · Operations

Turn every meeting into work that gets done.

Run Meeting insights in Maskin so your team gets meeting promises done while Maskin tracks commitments and checks follow-through.

Install this flow

The job

After a week, meeting promises are either done or have an owner and a date.

Replaces: Manual note-taking, copying action items into task lists, and checking back by hand.

Stays with your team: Decisions, judgment, and replies to people outside your company.

What your team keeps, what Maskin runs

Your team keepsMaskin runs
Deciding who owns each promiseFiles a task for every promise and suggests an owner
Deciding what to do with decisions and objectionsRecords decisions and objections
Choosing which written findings to act onFiles product, market, and customer findings with a speaker quote, marked first-hand or second-hand
Handling replies to outside buyersTells your team lead, or the workspace owner if there is no team lead, when a buyer reply is owed
Writing agendas by handDrafts internal meeting agendas from a template and adds open actions without overwriting a hand-written agenda

How the Flow runs

  1. When a Google Meet transcript is ready, Maskin processes the meeting straight away.
  2. Maskin creates one meeting record per meeting, even if there are multiple transcript sources. It links attendees to existing contacts and companies, and creates contacts only for outside attendees.
  3. A weekday sweep of your notetaker tool every two hours from 07:15 to 19:15 UTC picks up meetings with transcripts.
  4. At 07:17 UTC on weekdays, Maskin catches up on meetings marked “happened” that have a written reflection or attached transcript file.
  5. At 14:00 UTC each weekday, and in a review on Mondays, Maskin checks promises from the last 14 days and checks agreed next steps with outside parties against what happened. Before a meeting with the same company or people, it posts open commitments, objections, and decisions from the earlier meeting.
  6. A daily health check confirms the sweep ran, finds stale meetings, and fixes written findings that are not linked to their meeting.
  7. For scheduled internal meetings, Maskin drafts an agenda from the template and adds open actions from the last related meeting.

For each meeting, Maskin writes an analysis based on meeting type, files tasks, decisions, objections, and written findings, and records a follow-up date and a “My confidence” section. Missing information appears under “What is NOT captured.” Maskin does not invent names or look up attendees on social networks.

What you need to run it

  • Install the Flow. Nothing is connected when you install it.
  • Google Meet is optional. Connect it for processing when a transcript is ready.
  • Google Calendar is optional. Connect it for agenda drafts based on scheduled internal meetings.
  • A notetaker tool is optional. Connect it for the weekday transcript sweep.
  • Connect whichever of those tools you use. If you connect none, you can add a written reflection to a meeting. Without a transcript, recording, or reflection, the meeting stays at “happened” and says it has no recording.
  • First, you will see a meeting record with its analysis, attendees, tasks, decisions, written findings, follow-up date, and confidence section.

Trust surface

  • Human gates: Your team handles outside replies. Maskin does not send or draft anything outside your company.
  • Your data stays yours: You connect your own tools after install. Nothing ships connected.
  • Fails safely: Maskin does not mark a meeting processed without a transcript, recording, or written reflection. It notes missing information instead of inventing names, and never overwrites an agenda written by hand.

Example first week

Example for fictional Brightlane, project software for architects.

Day 1: Brightlane connects Google Meet and Google Calendar.

A customer says, “We need the room schedule before we can approve the plan.”

Maskin records the customer’s objection and quotes the speaker.

Maskin files the promised room schedule follow-up as a task and suggests an owner.

Day 2: The team reviews the task and sets its follow-up date.

Before the next meeting with that customer, Maskin posts the open commitment and objection.

The team sends the room schedule and marks the commitment done.
