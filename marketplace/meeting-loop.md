# Turn every meeting into work that gets done.
When a meeting ends, Maskin writes it up, files the tasks, decisions and insights, hands each task to the right person or agent, and follows up until every promise is kept.
Sign in or sign up, then confirm the loop in the Maskin app.
1. A meeting ends
The Meeting Manager picks up the transcript from whatever you have connected, such as Google Meet, Google Calendar or a notetaker tool.
2. It writes up what happened
A short summary shaped by the type of meeting, with the people and companies who were there linked to the records you already have.
3. It files the work
Tasks, decisions and insights are created and linked to the meeting, and each task goes to the right person or agent.
4. It follows up
It keeps track of every promise, yours and theirs, and checks until each one is done, cancelled or handed to someone else.
It also drafts an agenda for new internal meetings, catches meetings that were never processed, and checks every day that nothing is stuck.
It acts on its own for routine work. It asks you first before anything goes outside your company, changes a commercial commitment, or deletes or merges important records.
*Set up:* after you install, connect your own meeting and calendar tools. The loop works with what you connect.
For teams whose meetings end with notes nobody acts on.

## What this loop is built with
Named the same on every loop page so the shape is portable. Below the fold on purpose: the loop is what closes, the primitives are just the parts.
Agent
The Meeting Manager, one role with a job description, the tools it can use, and the gates it has to pass before its work leaves your workspace.
Triggers
Six triggers start the work: a meeting transcript arriving, a sweep of your notetaker every two hours on weekdays, a catch-up for meetings that got stuck, a follow-up check each weekday, a daily health check, and an agenda draft when an internal meeting is scheduled.
Skills
Eight skills carry the detailed rules: how meetings are owned and followed up, how each meeting type is summarised, how people and tasks are filed, and how internal meetings are run. Four are shared working rules the other loops use too.
Your tools
The loop does not ship with a meeting tool. You connect Google Meet, Google Calendar or a notetaker yourself, under your own access.
