How it works

Hire, brief, approve.

  1. Hire.

    Tell the Maskin Team which job eats the most time on a short call. That job is your first hire.

  2. Brief.

    The Maskin Team sets the agent up in the tools you already use, so nobody has to switch systems, and briefs it with you in plain words. It starts in draft-only mode: it drafts, your team reviews everything.

  3. Approve.

    As the first job proves itself, you decide how much the agent may do without asking. You can pull it back any time.

Questions

Questions we hear.

Does this fit a business our size?

It works for a team of five and a team of fifty. Start with one job in one team.

Do we need to be technical?

No. You explain the job in plain words. The Maskin Team does the setup.

What if it gets something wrong?

It cannot send anything without approval, so mistakes are caught before a customer sees them. That is why every job starts in draft-only mode.

Is this just a chatbot?

No. A chatbot answers questions. A Maskin agent does the job and then asks you to approve it.

What does it cost?

Two parts, both at a fixed price agreed before you start. First, setup by the Maskin Team: DKK 9,995 for a one-day setup of your first jobs, or EUR 9,995 for a fuller build over a month. Then one monthly price for the whole team, not per person. The two monthly plans are listed above and exclude VAT. Invoicing currency is confirmed on the call.
