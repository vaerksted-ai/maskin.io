04 · Use cases

High-quality outcomes matter more than ever post-AI.

Four ways teams run Maskin as loops.

01 · Shared business context

An LLM wiki your agents live in, not a doc store they visit.

Every meeting note, deal, and decision lands as a typed object your agents read and write back — context compounds instead of decaying. Maskin doesn't bolt a wiki onto the product; the workspace is the wiki, MCP-native and model-agnostic, yours to self-host.

02 · Product discovery

Making sense of the data. Making sure the team builds the right things.

Transcripts, tickets, and feedback become shaped bets with a close condition — not one more dashboard. Because every agent writes back to shared context, discovery doesn't restart from a blank doc each cycle.

03 · Go-to-market

From LinkedIn touch to booked call.

Prospecting, qualification, and follow-up run as one loop on the context the whole team works from — so a handoff never loses the thread. Humans keep the judgement on the calls that matter.

04 · SEO inbound marketing

Pages that keep compounding after they publish.

The loop closes on the page ranking, not the page shipping — and search signal feeds straight back into what gets written next.
