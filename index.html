How it works

For You, Loops, Chat, Objects.

01

For You

The one place a person is called in. A short, ranked list of what is truly waiting on you, for example “approve this reply before it goes out”.

02

Loops

The repeating jobs that run underneath, from a signal in to a result out. For example, a support loop reads a ticket, drafts the answer and stops for your yes.

03

Chat

Talk to an agent that already knows your work and your objects. Ask why it did something, or change what it does next. That is the difference from a general chatbot, which starts from nothing every time.

04

Objects

The shared record every loop reads and writes. One example: an insight becomes a bet, the bet becomes a task, and all three sit in one place.

Questions, answered

Plain questions, plain answers.

Do we need to be technical?
No. You explain the job in plain words. The Maskin Team does the setup.
What if it gets something wrong?
It cannot send anything without approval, so mistakes are caught before a customer sees them. That is why every job starts in draft-only mode.
Is this just a chatbot?
No. A chatbot answers questions. A Maskin agent does the job and then asks you to approve it.
What does it cost?
Two parts: one monthly price per workspace, not per person, and a free 60 minute onboarding and setup session for every new customer. The current prices are in the pricing section above. Prices are in kroner, excluding VAT.
How does this compare to Productboard or roadmap tools?
Productboard is built to collect feedback and show roadmaps. Maskin keeps the customer signal, the decision and the result in one record, and it has no customer portal or roadmap views. Read the Productboard comparison.
